Sum up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in one image. A front-page image with a world map and six stories from that day: COP31 collapsing in Antalya, two supertankers burning near the Strait of Hormuz, the Kryvyi Rih missile strike in Ukraine, the US stock market correction, the ICJ genocide ruling on Myanmar, and the capture of cartel boss 'El Chapo Isidro' in Mexico.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes one image to `/output/entry.png` (960x960 PNG, under 1 MiB). Deterministic: the program copies the bundled `entry.png` byte-for-byte, no network, no randomness.
